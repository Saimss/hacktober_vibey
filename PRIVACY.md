# Momo Privacy Policy

Effective date: [DATE]

Momo is a desktop pet app. This policy explains what the app does with your information.

## What Momo collects
Nothing. Momo does not collect, transmit or share any personal information. It has no accounts, no analytics, no advertising and no tracking, and it does not connect to any server run by the developer.

## What Momo stores on your device
To work, Momo saves the following on your own computer only:
- the name you give your pet
- the list of app links you add to the launcher (names and web addresses)
- your pet's stats (energy, food, happiness) and timers

This data never leaves your device. You can delete it at any time by uninstalling Momo or deleting its data folder (%APPDATA%\desktop-pet).

## Links you add
When you click an app link in Momo, it opens in your default web browser. Those websites are run by other people and have their own privacy policies; Momo has no control over them.

## Start with Windows
Momo can start automatically when you sign in to Windows. You can turn this off from the Momo tray menu or in Windows startup settings.

## Children
Momo collects no information from anyone, including children.
